We build intelligence that can act in the physical world and account for every decision.

Neural systems sense. Symbolic systems prove. Together they let robots and enterprise agents operate when the world is incomplete, noisy, or adversarial.

Until now, teams chose between scale and assurance. Our neurosymbolic stack changes that, uniting perception, planning, and formal guarantees so autonomous systems stay fast without going opaque.
